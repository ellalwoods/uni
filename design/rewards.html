Rewards for real milestones

Three ways to show the same milestones. Each concept has the moment you unlock something (tap Replay to watch it again) and the place you collect them. The milestones, listed at the bottom, are all earned by adding notes, studying, getting cards right or scoring well, spaced out across the semester.

1. The Mirror Ball Wardrobe

The disco ball is DuoDisco’s character. Each milestone unlocks a new look for it, and the one you choose shows in the header, the sidebar and on the Hello screen.

The unlock moment
The wardrobe

2. The Record Crate

Each milestone presses a record with its own cover and title. They line up in a crate in the order you earned them, so the crate tells the story of your semester.

The unlock moment
The crate

3. Your Dance Floor

A 5×5 floor, one tile per milestone. Each one lights up for good in its own colour. By the end of semester the whole floor is lit.

The floor

The milestones

About 22 across a 13-week semester: roughly one or two a week, each for real work. Nothing for setting up, and no daily streaks.
