Glasshouses, redesigned

This keeps your idea of a glasshouse for each course, a habitat inside each one, a seed packet for each of the 13 weeks, and the weeks growing in the vines across the front. I rebuilt everything around one question: can you tell where you are, and what to tap next, in under a second?

The content shown is made up.

  • 1
    One job per screenHome picks a course. A glasshouse shows its 13 weeks. Tapping a week opens it in a sheet. Studying fills the screen.
  • 2
    Weeks never moveWeeks 1–7 run along the top of the vines and weeks 8–13 along the bottom, always in the same places, so you find a week by where it is.
  • 3
    One look per stateFruit means planted. A glowing bud means its notes are in and it's ready to plant. A small white blossom means sealed, with no notes yet.
  • 4
    Each glasshouse has its own light and flowerOranges in gold, wisteria in lilac, roses in blush, camellias in mint. You know which course you're in before reading anything.
  • 5
    Controls stay in the same placesBack is always top left, the seed packet stack is always on the shelf, and the main action is always at the bottom.
