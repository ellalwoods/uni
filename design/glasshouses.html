Your glasshouses

Each course is a glasshouse. On the home screen you see them from outside through fogged glass, with the habitat inside just about visible. Open one and it has its own light habitat, with a shelf of 13 seed packets for its 13 weeks. A packet stays sealed until you've added that week's notes, then it's ready to plant. How the pieces fit

In the appWhat it is
A glasshouseOne course. You can have one or more, and you add another with the + button.
Its habitatEach course's glasshouse has its own theme (an orangery, an alpine house, a succulent house, a lily house), so they always look different.
A seed packetOne week. There are 13 on the shelf. The intensive course can use a different layout once you know how it runs.
Sealed packetNo notes added for that week yet, so it can't be planted.
Ready packetNotes are in. The packet shows the concepts (seeds) written from your notes, and you plant it when you're ready.
Planted weekIt grows in the glasshouse as you learn its concepts. You can study it any time with flashcards, a quiz or a summary.
